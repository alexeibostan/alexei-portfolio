Key Decisions in This Direction

  • Single-page scroll — your story flows as one narrative, no page jumps breaking the experience
  • Projects as mini case studies (problem → craft → impact) instead of flat cards — reinforces your brand
  • Skills as layered meaning (what/how/why) instead of a badge wall — shows depth of thinking
  • Career timeline is horizontal, scroll-animated — feels modern, saves vertical space
  • Strikethrough "merged" hero moment — immediately communicates your differentiator
  • Nav maps to the narrative: Story → Work → Craft → Connect (not generic Home/About/Skills)

Connect — Closing the Conversation

"Let's build something that matters" echoes the hero's gold italic — bookending the experience. The subtitle speaks directly to your ideal collaborators (PMs, designers, engineers). Social links are clean bordered buttons, not scattered icons. Resume download sits subtle below. The copyright line doubles as the footer — no need for a separate footer component in a single-page scroll.

Animation

  • Scroll entry: Section number fades in, then headline types or fades word-by-word, then subtitle, then link buttons stagger in from below
  • Hover on link buttons: Border brightens, subtle background fill appears, slight lift
  • Bookend moment: The word "matters" uses the same gold italic treatment as "build" and "use" in the hero — a visual callback that closes the loop

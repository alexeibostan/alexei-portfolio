Option C — Subsection at the Bottom of Craft

AI lives below the concentric rings as a "Currently Exploring" subsection within Craft. More compact, keeps the nav at 4 items. The trade-off: AI gets less visual prominence — it's positioned as an interest within your broader toolkit rather than a defining pillar of your identity.

My recommendation: Option B. AI is a big enough part of your story — you're both a power user of AI tools AND building an AI product — that it deserves its own section. Tucking it into Craft undersells it. The "Building WITH / Building IN" split is a strong narrative device that few portfolios have.

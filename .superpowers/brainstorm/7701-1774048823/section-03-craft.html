Concentric Rings — Interactive Skill Map

Three concentric rings represent depth of thinking: outer ring (What I Build With — technologies), middle ring (How I Build — methodologies), inner core (Why I Build — user empathy). Hovering a skill pill on the rings populates the detail panel on the right with experience years, category, and a personal context line tying that skill to your work.

Interaction & Animation

  • Scroll entry: Rings draw themselves outward from the center — inner core appears first, then middle ring expands, then outer ring. Skill pills fade in once their ring is drawn.
  • Hover on skill pill: Pill glows brighter, its ring subtly pulses. Detail panel on the right smoothly updates with that skill's info. A faint line connects the hovered pill to the detail panel.
  • Ring rotation: Outer ring very slowly rotates (60s full rotation) for subtle motion. Middle ring rotates opposite direction at half speed. Inner core stays still.
  • Default state: On load, React is pre-selected so the detail panel isn't empty.
  • Languages: Sits below the detail panel as a compact card — reinforces the multilingual identity without competing with technical skills.
  • Mobile: Rings scale down or collapse into a categorized vertical list (What / How / Why headers). Tapping a skill expands its detail inline. Languages card stays as-is below.

Why This Works for Your Brand

Most portfolios list skills as a flat grid of badges — it says "I know these things" but nothing about how you think. The concentric model tells a story: the tools are just the outer layer, the methodologies shape how you use them, but at the core is user empathy. A technical lead scanning this immediately sees someone who thinks beyond the stack.
